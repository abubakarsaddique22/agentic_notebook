# TimeHouse Shipping Policy

TimeHouse delivers across Pakistan through TCS and Leopards Courier.

## Delivery times
- Lahore: 1 to 2 working days.
- Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan: 2 to 4 working days.
- Other cities and towns: 3 to 6 working days.

## Charges
- Standard delivery charge is PKR 250 per order.
- Delivery is free on orders above PKR 10,000.

## Payment
- We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) only.
- Please keep the exact amount ready; riders may not carry change.

## Tracking
- A tracking number is shared by SMS once the parcel is handed to the courier.
- Customers can ask the support assistant for the status of their own order using the order ID.
