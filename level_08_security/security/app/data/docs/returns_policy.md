# TimeHouse Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
- Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
- The watch or wallet must be unused, with original tags, box and warranty card.

## Not returnable
- Items with scratches, resized straps or removed protective film.
- Items bought during clearance sales.

## Refund process
1. Customer requests a return through support with the order ID.
2. Our team arranges a courier pickup.
3. After the item passes inspection (2 to 3 working days), the refund is sent by bank transfer or JazzCash/Easypaisa.
4. Delivery charges are not refunded unless the item was faulty or wrong.

## Refund limits
- Refunds are never more than the amount the customer actually paid for that order.
- Refunds above PKR 5,000 must be approved by a store manager.
