# TimeHouse Warranty

## Coverage
- All watches carry a 1-year warranty on the movement (machine).
- Wallets carry a 3-month warranty on stitching.

## Not covered
- Glass, strap, buckle and battery.
- Water damage on watches that are not marked water resistant.
- Damage from drops, misuse or repair by a third party.

## How to claim
- Contact support with the order ID and a short video of the problem.
- Warranty repairs take 10 to 15 working days.
