# Demonstration Company Travel Policy

## Flight class
For company flights with flying time below 8 hours, employees must book Economy class. Business class requires prior director approval. Exceptions must be documented.

## Hotel rules
Employees may select hotels up to 4-star category. Illustrative international accommodation allowance is INR 12,000 per night. Exceeding the limit requires written manager approval.

## Reimbursement
Flight tickets, hotel expenses, airport taxis, and pre-approved local transport are reimbursable with valid itemized receipts. Reimbursements must be submitted within 15 days after travel.

## Travel approval
All international business travel requires manager approval before tickets are purchased. Employees should submit the trip purpose and estimated costs before booking.

## Travel preparation
Employees should check passport validity, visa requirements, destination weather, and meeting schedule ahead of travel.
